# Spellstorm Privacy Policy

**Keith Burgun Games** · Effective November 9, 2026
Contact: [keithburgungames@gmail.com](mailto:keithburgungames@gmail.com)

This policy covers the Spellstorm digital game on all platforms: Steam (Windows, Linux and Steam Deck), itch.io, Android, Reddit and the web version. In short, Spellstorm stores your progress on your own device. It contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your data.

## What stays on your device

Your profile, settings, unlocked achievements, puzzle progress, match history and replays are saved locally on your device. We do not receive any of it unless you use one of the optional features below.

## Optional features that send data

**Account sync (optional).** If you choose to sign in, you give an email address and a password. The account lets you keep your progress in sync across devices. We store your email address and your save data: profile, achievements, settings and match history (replays are not uploaded). This runs on [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy), which acts as our data processor. Passwords are handled by Supabase's authentication service, and we never see them. If you don't sign in, nothing is sent.

**Online play.** Online matches connect the two players directly (peer-to-peer). To find each other, both games briefly contact a public matchmaking server run by [PeerJS](https://peerjs.com). That server, and the other player, can see your IP address, as with any direct connection. Only game moves are exchanged. There is no chat and no profile sharing beyond your chosen wizard and display name.

**Steam features (Steam version only).** On Steam, your save file is kept in Steam Cloud, and Steam receives your achievements and Rich Presence (for example "In a match"). [Valve's privacy policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/) governs this data. We see only the aggregate statistics that Steam gives every developer.

**Reddit version.** When you play Spellstorm inside a Reddit post while logged in, your save (profile, settings, achievements and match history) is stored with your Reddit account on Reddit's Developer Platform, so your progress follows you across devices. The game uses only your Reddit user ID and username for this. [Reddit's privacy policy](https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy) covers Reddit's handling of that data. The Reddit version has no online play, account login, update checks or match-log sending.

**Update checks (itch.io and Android versions only).** Before telling you about a new version, the game asks GitHub for the latest release number. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any web request. The Steam version leaves updates to Steam.

**Fonts.** Some versions load their typefaces from Google Fonts, and Google sees your IP address when it serves them.

**Problem reports and match logs (only when you send them).** Options › "Report a problem" writes a report for you to send. It contains your description and, if you leave the box ticked, game details: version, mode, the recent game log, your device type and screen size. On most versions the report opens in your own email app, addressed to us, and you choose whether to send it. In the web version, "Send report" and "Send to Keith" (on a finished match) deliver the report or match log straight to the developer. Nothing is sent unless you press the button.

## What we don't do

- No advertising, and no ad networks.
- No analytics, telemetry or tracking.
- No sale or rental of your data, and no sharing of it beyond the services named above.
- No in-game purchases or payment details. Steam handles purchases.

## Your choices and rights

You can play without an account. To delete your account and the save data we hold, email [keithburgungames@gmail.com](mailto:keithburgungames@gmail.com) from the address on the account, and we will delete it within 30 days. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or for corrections. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have further rights, and we honor them on request. To remove your local data, uninstall the game or reset your profile in Options.

## Children

Spellstorm is suitable for all ages, but account sync is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

Account data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored by Supabase with access controls.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the game's news.
