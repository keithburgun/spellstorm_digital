How to Win	如何获胜
You can win at Spellstorm by gatherin’ up 8 Storm Shards, or by bringing your opponent to zero health. These resources are shown… here!	在法术风暴中，收集8块风暴碎片，或者把对手的生命打到零，你就赢了。这些资源都显示在……这里！

The Storm Cloud	风暴云

Weather Cards	天气牌
Each turn a new Weather card’ll pop out, doing some stuff. Luckily this one’s just a nice breeze, givin’ all of us some resources, but some aren’t so nice.	每回合都会冒出一张新的天气牌，带来各种效果。幸好这张只是宜人的微风，给我们大家一些资源，不过有些天气可没这么友好。
Playing Cards	出牌
Oh yeah! You can play cards from your hand. Each turn you play one, face-down. Then they get revealed at the same time, and do some magic stuff!	对了！你可以从手牌中出牌。每回合面朝下出一张。然后大家同时展示，施展魔法！
There’s like an elemental rock paper scissors thing: Fire beats Earth, Earth beats Water and Water beats Fire. If you counter the opponent, you draw a card.	这有点像元素版的石头剪刀布：火克土，土克水，水克火。如果你克制了对手，就能抽一张牌。
(Drawing cards is a big deal actually in Spellstorm, I’ll tell you why in a bit.)	（在法术风暴里，抽牌其实非常重要，等会儿告诉你为什么。）
Hell yeah! Earth beats Water - you just countered Eve’s card. She’s gonna be pissed. Anyway, you drew a card.	太好了！土克水——你刚刚克制了伊芙的牌。她肯定气坏了。总之，你抽了一张牌。
Right now you have the Initiative token, so your card resolves first. Initiative matters for a few things, especially Fire cards (my favorite).	现在你拥有先手标记，所以你的牌先结算。先手在一些情况下很重要，尤其是对火牌（我的最爱）。
Eve’s Block card let her take the Initiative, so next turn, her card will resolve first.	伊芙的格挡牌让她夺得了先手，所以下回合她的牌会先结算。
Blast Score	冲击分
Each battle has four turns, and then you check how many cards each player has in hand. Whoever has more cards in hand gains 2 Storm Shards!	每场战斗有四个回合，然后比较每名玩家的手牌数量。手牌多的一方获得2块风暴碎片！
Actually, it’s a little more complicated than that, because cards with discard effects (they have a white border) aren’t counted towards your total (we call this your Blast Score).	其实还要再复杂一点，因为带有弃置效果的牌（它们有白色边框）不计入你的总数（我们称之为你的冲击分）。
Derby’s Ultimate	德比的绝招

You can use your Ultimate whenever the Ultimate icon is on an action card, and you have the required amount of mana. Usually good to do it as much as you can!	只要出的牌上有绝招图标，并且你有足够的法力，就可以使用绝招。通常能用就尽量用！
